Official Club Calendar & Fixtures

Never Miss a Kickoff

Subscribe once to the official SF Fog RFC calendar. Matches, practice sessions at Kimbell Playground & Crocker Amazon, socials, and tournament fixtures automatically sync and update across Apple, Google, and Outlook calendars.

Choose your calendar app

1

iPhone & Mac

  • Tap Subscribe in Apple Calendar below.
  • Confirm Subscribe when Calendar opens.
  • Fixtures, pitch changes and kickoff times update automatically.

Google Calendar

  • Tap Add to Google Calendar below.
  • Click Add to confirm the Fog calendar.

Outlook & Others
