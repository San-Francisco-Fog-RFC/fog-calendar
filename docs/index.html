Official Club Calendar

Never Miss a Kickoff

Subscribe once to the SF Fog RFC calendar. Matches, practices, socials, and tournament details sync automatically to your phone or desktop.

Subscribe (Apple / Outlook / iOS) › Add to Google Calendar › Open Interactive Calendar Portal →
📅 Quick Setup Instructions
  • 1
    iPhone & Mac: Tap Subscribe (Apple / Outlook) above and confirm. All schedule and venue updates sync automatically.
  • 2
    Google Calendar: Tap Add to Google Calendar, or in Google Calendar web go to Other calendars → + → From URL and paste the link.
  • 3
    Outlook & Others: Select Add calendar → Subscribe from web and paste the subscription URL.
