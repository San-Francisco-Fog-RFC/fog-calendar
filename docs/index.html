Official Club Calendar & Fixtures

Never Miss a Kickoff

Subscribe once to the official SF Fog RFC calendar. Matches, practice sessions at Kimbell Playground & Crocker Amazon, socials, and tournament fixtures automatically sync and update across Apple, Google, and Outlook calendars.

Subscribe (Apple / Outlook / iOS) › Add to Google Calendar › Open Interactive Calendar Portal →

Single source of truth: events.yml · View interactive agenda in the Calendar Portal · Questions: secretary@fogrugby.com

📅 Quick Setup Guide
  • 1
    iPhone & Mac: Tap Subscribe (Apple / Outlook) and confirm. Schedule, pitch, and kickoff updates sync automatically.
  • 2
    Google Calendar: Tap Add to Google Calendar, or in Google Calendar web select Other calendars → + → From URL and paste the link.
  • 3
    Outlook & Others: Select Add calendar → Subscribe from web and paste the subscription URL.
