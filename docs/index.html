Official Club Calendar & Fixtures

Never Miss a Kickoff

Subscribe once to the official SF Fog RFC calendar. Matches, practice sessions at Kimbell Playground & Crocker Amazon, socials, and tournament fixtures sync automatically across your devices.

Select your calendar app

Subscribe in Apple Calendar

  1. Tap Subscribe in Apple Calendar below.
  2. Confirm Subscribe when Calendar opens (on Mac, click Subscribe then OK).
  3. Matches, pitch updates, and kickoff times sync automatically.
Subscribe in Apple Calendar
  1. Tap Add to Google Calendar below while signed in to your Google account.
  2. Click Add to confirm — SF Fog RFC appears under Other calendars.
  3. On Android, open the Google Calendar app → Settings → SF Fog RFC and turn on Sync.
Add to Google Calendar
  1. Tap Add to Outlook below to subscribe in Outlook.com or Microsoft 365 on the web, then click Import.
  2. Using desktop Outlook instead? Copy the link below and choose Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
  3. Fixtures, venues, and kickoff changes update automatically.
Add to Outlook
  1. Click Copy Link below to copy the official iCalendar feed URL.
  2. In your calendar app, choose Add calendar, Subscribe from web, or From URL.
  3. Paste the link and save — schedule updates sync automatically.

Prefer to browse in your browser? Open the interactive agenda in the Calendar Portal.
