President's Dispatch

Preseason Is Here

Fog Family,

What an extraordinary start to our fall rugby calendar. Sunday's position calling ceremony was a moving tribute to the strength, dedication, and camaraderie that define San Francisco Fog RFC. Watching our 2026 Pathways rookies step forward and claim their positions was an unforgettable moment for the entire club.

I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to Alvin and every Pathways lead and coach who poured countless hours of patience, skill-building, and heart into this cohort over the last month. Our club grows stronger because of your selfless leadership.

A huge thank you as well to our dedicated board members who keep our club firing on all cylinders behind the scenes. From medkit refills and AirTag equipment management to beer bust fundraising, tournament logistics, and community partnerships—your work keeps the Fog flying.

Tonight, we officially kick off preseason training at Crocker Amazon. Whether you're a ten-year veteran or a fresh Pathways graduate, bring your boots, your mouthguard, and your heart. Let's build something special together this season.

Justin Melbourne
President, San Francisco Fog RFC
president@fogrugby.com

Pathways to Rugby

Congratulations Pathways Class of 2026!

Over the past four weeks, over twenty new athletes took the pitch to learn rugby laws, safe contact, set pieces, and the inclusive spirit that makes the Fog family unique. On Sunday, October 25, we concluded the course with our traditional Position Calling Ceremony.

The Pathways Class of 2026 following their official position calling ceremony.
The closing circle: welcoming our rookies into the active ranks of Fog ruggers.

From props and locks to flankers, halfbacks, centers, and wings—every rookie now has a rugby home. Special recognition goes to Alvin and our coaching leads for their tireless dedication in making this cohort one of our most successful rookie classes in club history.

Mandatory: Complete Your USA Rugby (CIPP) Registration

To participate in full-contact drills, scrimmages, and sanctioned matches, all players must be CIPP registered with USA Rugby through Rugby Xplorer. This provides critical insurance coverage and certifies your eligibility.

1. Log in or create an account at Rugby Xplorer.
2. Select Register > Participate in Rugby.
3. Search for club: San Francisco Fog RFC.
4. Select your role (Senior Player / Rookie) and complete registration.
