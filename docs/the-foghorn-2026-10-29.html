Preseason Is Here

Fog Family,

What an extraordinary start to our fall rugby calendar. Sunday's position calling ceremony was a moving tribute to the strength, dedication, and camaraderie that define San Francisco Fog RFC. Watching our 2026 Pathways rookies step forward and claim their positions was an unforgettable moment for the entire club.

I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to Alvin and every Pathways lead and coach who poured countless hours of patience, skill-building, and heart into this cohort over the last month. Our club grows stronger because of your selfless leadership.

Congratulations Pathways Class of 2026!

Over the past four weeks, over twenty new athletes took the pitch to learn rugby laws, safe contact, set pieces, and the inclusive spirit that makes the Fog family unique. On Sunday, October 25, we concluded the course with our traditional Position Calling Ceremony.

The Pathways Class of 2026 following their official position calling ceremony on the pitch.

From front row props and second row locks to loose forwards, scrum halves, fly halves, centers, and wings—every rookie now has a position and a rugby home. Special recognition goes to Alvin and our coaching leads for their dedication in making this cohort one of our most successful in club history!

Preseason Starts Today! Have You CIPP'd?

Mandatory: Complete Your USA Rugby (CIPP) Registration

To participate in contact drills, live scrums, scrimmages, and sanctioned matches, all players must be CIPP registered through USA Rugby / Rugby Xplorer. CIPP provides required player medical & liability insurance and certifies your official playing eligibility.

1. Log in or create an account at Rugby Xplorer.
2. Go to Register > Participate in Rugby.
3. Search club: San Francisco Fog RFC.
4. Select your player role and complete payment.
