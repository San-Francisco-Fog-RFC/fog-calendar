Fog Family,

Congratulations Pathways Class of 2026!

Over the past four weeks, over twenty new athletes took the pitch to learn rugby laws, safe contact, set pieces, and the inclusive spirit that makes the Fog family unique. On Sunday, October 25, we concluded the course with our traditional Position Calling Ceremony.

The Pathways Class of 2026 following their official position calling ceremony on the pitch.

From front row props and second row locks to loose forwards, scrum halves, fly halves, centers, and wings—every rookie now has a position and a rugby home. Special recognition goes to Alvin and our coaching leads for their dedication in making this cohort one of our most successful in club history!

Preseason Starts Today! Have You CIPP'd?

Tonight we officially kick off our fall rugby campaign under the lights at Crocker Amazon! Whether you are a ten-year veteran or a fresh Pathways graduate, it's time to lace up your boots. And remember: Thursdays mean team drinks at Pilsner Inn right after practice! 🍻

🏉 Tonight's Kickoff · Thursday, Oct 29
⏰ Practice Time: 7:45 PM – 10:00 PM (Sharp)
📍 Where: Crocker Amazon Park (Field 1, Geneva & Moscow)
🎒 Bring: Cleats (boots), mouthguard, water bottle & warm layers
🍻 Thursday Social: Pilsner Inn (225 Church St) right after practice! Meet up for pints, laughs, and the traditional Thursday third half!
📋 Mandatory USA Rugby (CIPP) Registration

To participate in contact drills, scrums, and sanctioned matches, all players must be CIPP registered through USA Rugby / Rugby Xplorer. This provides essential player medical and liability insurance.

1 Head to Rugby Xplorer (myaccount.rugbyxplorer.com.au)
2 Navigate to Register > Participate
3 Search: San Francisco Fog RFC
4 Choose your player role & complete your registration dues

Fog Rugby Gear Exchange & Swap Meet

With our Pathway to Rugby course complete, we're heading into preseason, and it's time to start thinking about rugby gear. In partnership with The Mothership and St. Anthony's, we're kicking off preseason with a gear exchange!
