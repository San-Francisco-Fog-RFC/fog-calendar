Preseason Is Here
G'day team. Pathway to Rugby wraps up this week, preseason practice kicks off tonight at Crocker Amazon, and RuckTacular San Diego is just six weeks away.
First off, a massive thank you to Alvin and all our Pathways leads, buddy coaches, and drills leaders who dedicated their Tuesday and Thursday nights to introducing dozens of new players to rugby. You created a welcoming, inclusive space, and we're so proud to see so many rookies transition straight onto the pitch for preseason.
Huge gratitude also to our Board Members who have been working relentlessly behind the scenes — getting field permits locked down, equipment restocked, finances aligned, and planning our biggest community drives of the season.
Two asks from me: book your San Diego travel and tickets now if you haven't (including the official Scrum Dump afterparty announced below!), and check out Lifting the Fog, our new way to nominate and recognise teammates who elevate our club.
See you on the pitch tonight.
With You & Roll Fog Roll
Justin Melbourne
President, San Francisco Fog RFC

MANDATORY REQUIREMENT
Have You Registered with USA Rugby (CIPP)?

You must be registered with USA Rugby via Rugby Xplorer before you can participate in full-contact drills or matches. It takes about five minutes and provides essential injury and liability coverage.

Gear Swap Q&A
Q: What type of gear are you looking for?

Any rugby-related gear (shorts, jerseys, training shirts, cleats/boots) in good condition.

Q: How does the swap work?

Station 1 has free donated gear (priority to rookies!). Station 2 is for gear traded or sold directly between members.
