Preseason Is Here

Fog Family,

What an extraordinary start to our fall rugby calendar. Sunday's position calling ceremony was a moving tribute to the strength, dedication, and camaraderie that define San Francisco Fog RFC. Congratulations Pathways Class of 2026!

Over the past four weeks, over twenty new athletes took the pitch to learn rugby laws, safe contact, set pieces, and the inclusive spirit that makes the Fog family unique. On Sunday, October 25, we concluded the course with our traditional Position Calling Ceremony.

The Pathways Class of 2026 following their official position calling ceremony on the pitch.

From front row props and second row locks to loose forwards, scrum halves, fly halves, centers, and wings—every rookie now has a position and a rugby home. Special recognition goes to Alvin and our coaching leads for their dedication in making this cohort one of our most successful in club history!

Preseason Starts Tonight! Have You CIPP'd?

📋 Mandatory USA Rugby (CIPP) Registration

Before stepping onto the pitch for full-contact training or matches, every player must register with USA Rugby via Rugby Xplorer (formerly known as CIPP). Your registration covers mandatory catastrophic player insurance and official club roster eligibility for the 2026–2027 season.

STEP 1 Log in or create your profile at Rugby Xplorer.
STEP 2 Select Register and search for San Francisco Fog RFC.
STEP 3 Select Senior Player role and choose Northern California RFU.
STEP 4 Complete dues payment, sign the digital waivers, and forward confirmation to VP.

Fog Rugby Gear Exchange & Swap Meet

📅 Saturday, November 7, 2026 · 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM
📍 The Mothership Bar · 3152 Mission St (Bernal Heights), San Francisco (21+)

With Pathways complete and preseason underway, it's time to gear up! In partnership with The Mothership and St. Anthony's Foundation, we're hosting our fall gear swap. Swap or donate boots, shorts, and pads, drop off warm clothing for St. Anthony's drive, enjoy Mothership drink specials, and catch live drag performances by Foggers!

✓ Gear to Bring & Swap
• Cleats / boots (molded or studs)
• Rugby shorts & warm-up track pants
• Scrum caps & shoulder pads
• Training jerseys, pinnies, kit bags
• Casual clothes / shoes for St. Anthony's
✕ Please Leave at Home
• Used mouthguards (hygiene)
• Damaged / cracked safety equipment
• Severely torn or soiled garments
• Non-reusable athletic items
