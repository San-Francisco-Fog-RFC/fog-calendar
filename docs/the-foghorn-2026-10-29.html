Preseason Is Here
G'day team. Pathway to Rugby wraps up this week, preseason practice kicks off this Thursday at Crocker Amazon, and RuckTacular San Diego is just six weeks away.
First off, a massive thank you to Alvin and all our Pathways leads, buddy coaches, and drills leaders who dedicated their Tuesday and Thursday nights to introducing dozens of new players to rugby. You created a welcoming, inclusive space, and we're so proud to see so many rookies transition straight onto the pitch for preseason.
Huge gratitude also to our Board Members who have been working relentlessly behind the scenes — getting field permits locked down, equipment restocked, finances aligned, and planning our biggest community drives of the season.
Two asks from me: book your San Diego travel and tickets now if you haven't (including the official Scrum Dump afterparty announced below!), and check out Lifting the Fog, our new way to nominate and recognise teammates who elevate our club.
See you on the pitch Thursday night.
With You & Roll Fog Roll
Justin Melbourne
President, San Francisco Fog RFC

PRESEASON

Preseason Training Starts Thursday

Preseason is the best way to build your fitness and ball skills before competitive league play begins. It is 100% open to everyone — whether you just graduated from Pathways, haven't touched a rugby ball in years, or have played ten seasons.

Under the lights at Raymond Kimbell Playground.

Fog Rugby Gear Swap Party!

Nate Durand
Community Director

“With our Pathway to Rugby course complete, we’re heading into preseason, and it’s time to start thinking about rugby gear. In partnership with Mothership and St. Anthony’s, we’re kicking off preseason with a gear exchange to lower barriers for new players, celebrate our rookie class, and give back to our community. Join us to grab gear, donate clothing or shoes, and mix and mingle with the Fog family!”

📍 Mothership · 3152 Mission St, San Francisco, CA (21+)
🗓️ Saturday, Nov 7  |  ⏰ 2:00pm – 5:00pm
Featuring:
  • 👠 Drag performances by Foggers
  • 👕 St. Anthony’s donation drive (clothing & shoes)
  • 🍹 Mothership drink specials on the back patio
Gear Swap Q&A
Q: What type of gear are you looking for?

Any rugby-related gear (shorts, jerseys, training shirts, cleats/boots) in good condition.

Q: How does the swap work?

Station 1 has free donated gear (priority to rookies!). Station 2 is for gear traded or sold directly between members.

Register with USA Rugby (CIPP)

You must be registered with USA Rugby (via Rugby Xplorer) before taking the pitch for contact drills or preseason matches. Takes about five minutes and provides essential liability/injury coverage.
