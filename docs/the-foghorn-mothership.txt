PRESIDENT'S UPDATE · JUSTIN MELBOURNE
======================================================================
70 CIPP'd Ruggers & Taking 3 Sides to RuckTacular!

Fog Family,
As of right now, we officially have 70 players CIPP-registered with USA Rugby and cleared for full-contact match play. That is a massive turnout for our club heading into our 25th season.

Because so many folks have stepped up and registered, the club has committed to taking THREE full playing sides to RuckTacular in San Diego (Dec 3–6). Everyone traveling gets solid pitch time.

BY THE NUMBERS:
* 70 — Players CIPP-registered with USA Rugby (cleared for contact)
* 35 — Returning veterans re-committed
* 28 — Pathways graduates on the senior match roster
* 17 — First-time Fog ruggers registered with the club

FUNDRAISING HIGHLIGHTS:
* 🍻 $2,100+ raised at our SF Eagle Beer Bust — thank you to everyone who poured beers and bought raffle tickets!
* 💜 $1,300+ contributed this past month by three individual donors — thank you for backing the club!

Justin Melbourne
President, San Francisco Fog RFC
president@fogrugby.com
