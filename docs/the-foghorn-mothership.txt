PRESIDENT'S DISPATCH & PRESEASON BY THE NUMBERS
======================================================================
Preseason Momentum: 70 CIPP’d & 3 Sides to RuckTacular!

Foggers,
The momentum heading into our 25th anniversary year is breathtaking. As of right now, we have crossed 70 players officially CIPP-registered with USA Rugby and cleared for full-contact match play!

Because of this massive turnout, the board and coaching staff have officially committed to fielding THREE playing sides at RuckTacular 2026 in San Diego! Everyone traveling gets heavy pitch time against premier IGR clubs.

2026–27 Preseason By The Numbers:
* 70 Players CIPP-registered with USA Rugby & cleared for contact
* 35 Returning veterans re-committed from our 2025–26 season
* 28 Pathways graduates now active on the senior match roster
* 17 First-time Fog ruggers officially registered with the club

💜 RECENT CLUB FUNDRAISING WINS:
* 🍻 $2,100+ raised at our Eagle Beer Bust — massive thanks to everyone who poured beers and bought raffle tickets!
* 💜 $1,300+ contributed this past month by three generous individual donors — thank you for backing the Fog!

Justin Melbourne
President, San Francisco Fog RFC
president@fogrugby.com
