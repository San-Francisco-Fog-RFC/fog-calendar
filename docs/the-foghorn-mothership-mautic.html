FEATURED EVENT · TODAY 2:00 – 5:00 PM

Fog Rugby Gear Exchange & Social at The Mothership

📹 INSTAGRAM REEL: Watch what gear to bring to today's exchange! Watch Reel ›

It’s time to gear up, connect, and celebrate! With Pathways complete and our preseason schedule in full flight, we’re gathering at The Mothership Bar (3152 Mission St, SF · 21+ only) for an afternoon of kit trades, community impact, and team bonding.

STATION 1 · FREE / DONATED KIT (ROOKIE PRIORITY)
Clean rugby kit generously donated by alumni and returning players. Rookies get first priority to gear up for the season at no cost!
STATION 2 · PLAYER-TO-PLAYER SWAPS & TRADES
Buy, sell, or trade cleats, warmups, shorts, and tour stash directly with your teammates. Cash, Venmo, or good old rugby trades welcome.

St. Anthony’s Community Drive: We’re partnering with St. Anthony’s Foundation (alongside Tenderloin Tessie’s) to support vulnerable neighbors. Please bring gently used warm jackets, sweatshirts, denim, sneakers, or unopened socks and toiletries.

Live Entertainment: Enjoy signature Mothership patio drink specials plus show-stopping live drag performances by talented Foggers throughout the afternoon!

FRONT-OFFICE DISPATCH · PRESIDENT JUSTIN MELBOURNE

Preseason Momentum: 70 CIPP’d & 3 Sides to RuckTacular

Foggers,

The momentum heading into our 25th anniversary year is breathtaking. As of right now, we have crossed 70 players officially CIPP-registered with USA Rugby and cleared for full-contact match play!

Because of this massive turnout, the board and coaching staff have officially committed to fielding THREE playing sides at RuckTacular 2026 in San Diego! Everyone traveling gets heavy pitch time against premier IGR clubs.
