COMMUNITY SOCIAL & GEAR SWAP

Join Us At The Mothership:
Fog Rugby Gear Exchange & Social

📍 Venue: The Mothership Bar · 3152 Mission St, San Francisco, CA 94110 (21+ · Bring ID)
⏰ Time: Today / Saturday, Nov 7 · 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM
✨ Highlights: Two gear stations, St. Anthony's clothing drive, drag performances, patio drink specials
📹 Watch What To Bring
Check out our latest Instagram Reel with all the gear details!
Watch Reel ›

{contactfield=firstname|default=Fog family}, it’s time to gear up and celebrate! With Pathways complete and our preseason schedule in full flight, we’re gathering at The Mothership Bar for an afternoon of camaraderie, kit swaps, and community impact.

Whether you have extra boots, training shorts, or jerseys sitting in your closet, or you’re a rookie looking to complete your rugby kit bag, we have two dedicated stations set up all afternoon:

🏉 Station 1 · Free / Donated Kit Rookie Priority
Clean rugby gear generously donated by alumni and returning players. Rookies get first priority to gear up for the season with boots, training tees, and shorts at zero cost!
🤝 Station 2 · Player-to-Player Swaps & Trades All Teammates
Buy, sell, or trade cleats, warmups, match jerseys, and tour stash directly with teammates. Bring down gear you want to pass on or barter!

We are also partnering with St. Anthony’s Foundation (alongside Tenderloin Tessie’s) for a community clothing drive. Please bring gently used warm jackets, sweatshirts, denim, sneakers, and unopened socks or personal hygiene items. Plus, enjoy live drag performances by Foggers and signature Mothership patio drink specials!

PRESIDENT'S DISPATCH

Preseason Momentum: 70 CIPP’d Ruggers & 3 Sides to RuckTacular!

Foggers,

The momentum heading into our 25th anniversary year is breathtaking. As of right now, we have crossed 70 players officially CIPP-registered with USA Rugby and cleared for full-contact match play!

Because of this massive turnout, the board and coaching staff have officially committed to fielding THREE playing sides at RuckTacular 2026 in San Diego! Everyone traveling gets heavy pitch time against premier IGR clubs.
