NOVEMBER IS TRANSGENDER AWARENESS MONTH
Transgender Awareness Week runs November 13–19, leading into Transgender Day of Remembrance on November 20. Our trans and nonbinary ruggers, alumni, and supporters are at the core of what makes the Fog the club it is. Every body is welcome on our pitch.
 
PRESIDENT'S UPDATE · JUSTIN MELBOURNE

We're a family of 71+ CIPP'd Ruggers!

Fog Family,

While putting this email together, it brought me so much joy to see all the amazing things folks are doing to progress the club, support each other, keep us safe, and create opportunities for our community.

Congratulations to all of our Pathway to Rugby participants on completing our course! We hope that you enjoyed it, learned a lot, and will keep up the sport. Following your very first match of the preseason (Sunday, November 15 vs USF), we will be holding a graduation ceremony for you at the Pilsner Inn (our home bar). Veteran players and supporters are warmly invited too, of course!

As of right now, we officially have 71 players CIPP-registered with USA Rugby and cleared for full-contact match play. That is a massive turnout for our club heading into our 25th season, and it has been so good seeing familiar faces return over the past few practices.

We're gearing up for an exciting long weekend in early December for RuckTacular in San Diego (Dec 3–6). Please remember to book your accommodation and travel so you can join us! The club has committed to taking THREE full playing sides, so there are plenty of match minutes available for everyone.

Recent Highlights Across The Club:

  • 🍻 ,100+ raised at our Eagle Beer Bust — thank you to Director of Fundraising Justice Dumlao and everyone who helped out on the day. An incredible team effort!
  • 💜 ,300+ donated this past month by three individual donors — I know that I've thanked each of you in person, but thank you again for backing the club!
  • 💧 New Hygienic Push-Top Water Bottles — a massive thank you to Nate Durand's parents for purchasing brand-new water bottles for our squad this season. Each carrier now holds 10 bottles, and hopefully we won't have any repeat of the Koala incident!
  • 🏉 Thank you Coach Alvin, Pathway to Rugby Leads, House Mothers, and the Board for all your hard work making Pathway to Rugby a success. There were 64 net-new signups this year, and 28 have already CIPP'd with us for the 2026–27 season!
